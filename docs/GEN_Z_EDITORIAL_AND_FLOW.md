# EGON coffee media — owner clarification, 12 November 2026

EGON is a distinctive coffee-information media brand for Gen Z: history, culture, useful insights, current industry developments and practical coffee knowledge. Product commerce is not the daily editorial mission. Teach one interesting, sourced thing, create curiosity and invite exploration. Keep the 70% animated education / 20% authentic video / 10% carousel-Stories mix; do not turn it into a hard-sell quota.

## Visual and editorial acceptance

- First two seconds: a clear question, observation or visual reveal. No generic greeting or brand-only intro.
- One useful discovery, beginner-friendly explanation, and a specific takeaway. Contemporary Indonesian, warm and energetic, without forced slang or talking down to viewers.
- Change visual beats roughly every 2–4 seconds through a purposeful reveal, close-up, character reaction, comparison, title cue or camera move. Narration and visuals must explain the same thing.
- Original consistent 3D identity, warm cream/purple/orange, soft dimensional lighting, good facial readability and deliberate composition. The original engineering Workbench render is a technical reference, not the visual release bar.
- Kinetic/doodle titles support the learning point. Neon accents stay restrained; do not make coffee education look like an unrelated gadget advertisement.
- CTA: save, share, ask a useful question or follow the coffee exploration. No invented scarcity, price, stock or aggressive purchase claim.
- Logo remains the owner's exact canonical file, composited locally without repainting. No model-generated replacement or distorted labels.
- History claims distinguish documented facts from legend. Trends state region, period and evidence scale. Public copy does not claim a physical EGON lab test.

Technical MP4 QA is necessary but does not establish visual quality. Review actual frames from every scene, typography, animation pacing and the final composite before marking a pilot visually acceptable. Human approval remains required before publication.
